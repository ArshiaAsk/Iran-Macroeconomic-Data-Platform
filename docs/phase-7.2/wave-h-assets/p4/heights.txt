Note on the first attempt (before the viewport-reset fix)
---------------------------------------------------------
The first `--full-height` run reported grouped heights (4637 x3, 4693 x3,
5248 x4), matching the nav groups. Cause: `scrollHeight` never reports less
than the current viewport height, and the grown viewport from the previous
shot persisted into the next page, flooring its measurement. Fixed by
resetting the viewport to the default height (with a short reflow wait)
before measuring each page; the per-page heights above are the corrected,
independent values. `correlation` (921 px) and `trade_energy` (958 px) are
genuinely short (empty-state pages), so they are only slightly above the
900 px viewport — not padded.

catalog footer, no blank padding).
